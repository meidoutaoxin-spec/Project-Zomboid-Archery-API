description=Archery mod for Project Zomboid Build 42.

description=[h1]Core Features[/h1]

description=* Craft a Primitive Bow and a Hunting Bow
description=* Craft wooden arrows (stone arrowheads) and metal arrows (metal arrowheads)
description=* Minecraft-style bow: no reload key, no ammo in the bow
description=* Arrows that hit zombies can be recovered from the corpse

description=[h1]How to play[/h1]

description=1. Craft arrows: Plank + Saw -> arrow shafts, knap Sharped Stone -> stone arrowheads, Scrap Metal -> metal arrowheads
description=2. Combine shaft + arrowhead to make arrows
description=3. Craft a bow and keep arrows in your inventory
description=4. Aim (hold right mouse or Ctrl) to draw the bow, left click to loose an arrow. Drawing never consumes an arrow, only firing does
description=5. Recovered arrows drop next to the corpse when a zombie dies

description=[h1]Multiplayer[/h1]

description=Works on dedicated servers. Arrow consumption and recovery are handled server-side.
